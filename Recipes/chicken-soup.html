Chiken Soup

Return to Home

I spent several years of my life working for a small Jewish community center in central Indiana. Every friday we hosted a meal for local students and community members, completely free of charge. My fridays were spent in the kitchen preparing soups, breads, and dinners. As such, this recipe has had several evolutions with ingredients being added and removed based on season and budget. The version laid out here is my current go to when I want an easy and delicious soup.

Ingredients

Broth

  • 1x Whole chicken cut up
  • 1x Large carrot
  • 1x Large turnip
  • 1x Large yellow onion
  • 1x Head of garlic
  • 2x Fennel - Just the stems and leaves, we will use the rest later on
  • 1x Sprig of rosemarry
  • 1x Sprig of thyme
  • 1x Sprig of sage
  • A few whole peppercorn

Add ins

  • 1x or 2x Chicken breast
  • 1x Large carrot
  • 1x Large turnip
  • 1x Large yellow onion
  • 2x Fennel - Here is where we use the bulb of the fennel!
  • Salt and Pepper - to taste

Directions

Broth

  1. Wash all of the veggies. No need to peel any of them! - This includes the onion and garlic!
  2. Coursely chop the carrots and turnips.
  3. Quarter the onion, with the skin still on.
  4. Carefully slice the garlic in half sideways (hamburger-style), with the skin on.
  5. Remove the stems from the fennel.
  6. Add all of the broth ingredients together in a large pot, and add enough water to cover everything.
  7. Bring to a full boil, and then reduce to a simmer.
  8. Simmer until the kitchen smells AMAZING - More importantly: until the chicken reaches 165 F
  9. Strain the broth and return to the pot.

Add Ins

  1. While the broth is simmering, bake the chicken breast seasoned with salt pepper and whatever herbs you like.
  2. Peel and dice the carrot, onion, and turnip.
  3. Cut the roots araw from the fennel bulbs and thinly slice them.

Putting it all together

  1. Once the broth is strained and the chicken breasts are cooked through, add the chicken and add-in ingredients to the broth.
  2. Slowly heat the soup until the new ingredients are warm all the way through.
  3. Enjoy!
Pro Tip: This soup goes REALLY well with Matzah Balls or Egg Noodles

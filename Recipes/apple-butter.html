Apple Butter

Return to Home

One of my favorite fall recipes, apple butter. As soon as the leaves start to change color, I am in the kitchen making this recipe. It is the perfect blend of sweetness and warm-spices.

Ingredients

Main

  • 5 to 6 Pounds apples. - A mix of your favorites! I use cosmic crisp and granny smith.
  • 1 Cup Apple cider
  • 1 Cup Brown sugar, firmly packed
  • 3/4 Cup Granulated sugar

Spices - To Taste!

  • Cinnamon
  • Cloves
  • Nutmeg

Directions

  1. Peel and core the apples. Then Chop them into small pieces.
  2. Add chopped apples and cider to a large pot and bring to a simmer over low to medium heat.
  3. Stir constantly until most of the moisture has left the apples. You should be able to smash the apples with no resistance.
  4. Using either a blender or a stick blender, turn the soft apples into an apple sauce consistency.
  5. Add the pureed apples, the sugars, and the spices back into the pot.
  6. Reduce the sauce over low heat for the next several hours, stirring at regular intervals.
  7. Once the apples have reduced to a pasty consistency, remove from the head and transfer to a smaller container.
  8. Allow the apple butter to cool in the fridge over night. It will thicken as it cools.
  9. Enjoy on your favorite bread!
